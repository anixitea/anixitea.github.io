Your data

What you listen to stays on your phone, and nothing about it is sent to me. If you sign in with Google, your shows, progress and bookmarks follow you to every phone, kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. There's no GlassCast server. The privacy policy has the details.

Also in GlassCast

Bookmarks with notes, new episode alerts, downloads for offline listening, Chromecast and Google TV, Android Auto, gPodder and Nextcloud sync, OPML import and export, a home screen widget, and its own interface on Google TV.

Get GlassCast.

Download the APK and open it on your phone or Google TV. The app checks for updates itself, so you only need to install it once.
