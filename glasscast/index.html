GlassCast

A podcast app for Android phones and Google TV. Free, with no ads.

Download for Android Privacy

Why

I started GlassCast after moving over from an iPhone, because I couldn't find an Android podcast app I actually liked using. The player takes its colors from the cover of whatever you're listening to, and so do the mini player and the tab bar.

What it does

  • Queue episodes with a swipe, read show notes, jump between chapters and follow along with transcripts
  • Change the speed, skip silences, boost quiet voices, and set a sleep timer that a shake restarts
  • Bookmark a moment in an episode and write a note on it
  • Discover shows based on the ones you already follow, worked out on your phone
  • New episode alerts, downloads for offline listening, and new episodes that download themselves on Wi-Fi
  • Cast to a Chromecast or Google TV, or use it in Android Auto
  • Sync with a gPodder or Nextcloud server, or bring your shows from another app with OPML
  • Sign in with Google to keep your shows, progress and bookmarks on every phone you use, kept in your own Google account
  • A home screen widget, and shortcuts on the app icon
  • Its own interface on Google TV, made for the remote

Your data

You don't need an account to use GlassCast, and nothing about what you listen to is sent to me. If you sign in with Google, your library is kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only GlassCast can open. There's no GlassCast server. The privacy policy has the details.

Installing

Download the latest APK from the releases page and open it on your phone or Google TV. The app checks that page for updates itself, so you only need to install it once.
