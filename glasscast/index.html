A third look: Glass.

The tab bar, the mini player and the player's controls turn into real glass that bends what's behind it, with light catching the edges. Apple's layout and colors, set in Inter. Pick Frosted for a soft frost, or Liquid for clearer glass.

Skips the ads
it's heard before.

Turn on Skip ads and GlassCast goes through your downloaded episodes on your phone. When the same ad turns up in another episode or another show, it's marked on the progress line and skipped while you listen, with Undo if it got one wrong.

No AI and no account, and nothing is uploaded. The first time a new ad runs, it plays.

Your data

What you listen to stays on your phone, and nothing about it is sent to me. If you sign in with Google, your shows, folders, progress and bookmarks follow you to every phone and TV, kept as one file in your own Google Drive. There's no GlassCast server. The privacy policy has the details.

Also in GlassCast

Bookmarks with notes, a listening history with your week in numbers, a tag on episodes you've heard before, new episode alerts, downloads for offline listening, Chromecast and Google TV, Android Auto, gPodder and Nextcloud sync, OPML import and export, a home screen widget, a player made for foldables, and its own interface on Google TV.

Get GlassCast.

Download the APK and open it on your phone or Google TV. The app checks for updates itself, so you only need to install it once.
