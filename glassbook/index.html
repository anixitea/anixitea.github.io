GlassBook

A free audiobook player for Android. Point it at a folder and it finds your books, with their covers and chapters. No ads.

Get it on GitHub Privacy policy

Your books

  • M4B, MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC and the rest. A book split into many files becomes one book, with the chapters from the file.
  • A new book gets its cover, description and details from Audible's catalog by itself, when it's plainly the same book. You can pick another match or a cover from your photos.
  • Collections, the way you'd group apps on a home screen, and a grid you can pinch bigger or smaller.

Listening

  • The player takes its colors from the cover of the book you're in.
  • Jump between chapters, change the speed from 0.5x to 2x, skip silences, boost quiet voices, and set a timer for some minutes or the end of the chapter.
  • Bookmarks, a daily listening goal with your streak, and a history of what you've played, day by day.
  • A home screen widget, Android Auto and the lock screen.

Your Google account, if you want it

Sign in with Google and your library follows you to every phone you sign in on: where you are in each book, your bookmarks, your collections and your Want to Listen list. You can also keep the books themselves in your own Google Drive, in a GlassBook folder. On another phone they play straight away and download as they go.

It all stays in your own Drive. There's no GlassBook server. The privacy policy has the details.
